# cupano Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

cupano is a free browser extension made by an individual developer, not a company. It has no servers of its own, and the developer never receives, stores or sees any of your data.

When you click the cupano icon and press Analyze, cupano reads the text of the tab you are on, such as a coffee product page or a search results page. That text, together with your language, country and any taste preferences you entered, is sent directly from your browser to the AI provider you chose, Google (Gemini API) or OpenAI, using your own API key. From that point the data is handled by that provider under its own terms: Google's privacy policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy and OpenAI's at https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy. cupano does not read any page unless you start an analysis on it.

To check whether a bean is sold out in your country, cupano looks up your country from your IP address using api.country.is, with get.geojs.io as a fallback. The result is kept in your browser for 12 hours. If you pin a country in the settings, this lookup is not made.

Your API key, settings and preferences are stored only in your browser. The key is sent only to the provider it belongs to. Analysis results are cleared when you close the browser.

cupano does not collect analytics, does not track you, and does not sell or share your data with anyone. You can remove everything it stored by clearing the key in the settings or uninstalling the extension.
